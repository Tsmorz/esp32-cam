      Sleep nowMove the camera to wake it

      Asleep, the camera uses little power but can't be reached. Moving it wakes it in about five seconds. A timelapse keeps taking photos. Needs the motion sensor with its wake wire.

      Password

      On this device—

      Needed to watch and to change settings. It's remembered only in this browser. Forgot it? While the camera is on, hold its BOOT button for 5 seconds, until the light blinks fast, then let go: it restarts without a password and you choose a new one.

      Camera password

      Enter the password you chose when you set up the camera. Forgot it? Hold the camera's BOOT button for 5 seconds, then let go.
